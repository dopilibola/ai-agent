Privacy Policy

Assistant for Maskan, a grave-care service in Tashkent, operating on Telegram and WhatsApp. Last updated: 6 September 2026.

What we collect

  • Contact identifiers: your Telegram chat id or WhatsApp phone number, profile name and @username.
  • Message content: the text you send, transcriptions of your voice notes, and photos you send us.
  • Details you provide for the service: your name and phone number, the name of the deceased, the cemetery and grave details.
  • Order data: the service chosen, the amount and the payment status.

We never see or store your card details; payment happens on the payment provider's own page.

Why we use it

To answer your questions and quote prices from our catalogue, to place and fulfil an order, to let a human colleague continue the conversation when needed, and to improve the assistant's answers.

Who else processes it

  • Meta (WhatsApp) and Telegram — message delivery.
  • Google (Gemini) — generating replies and transcribing voice notes; message content is sent there for processing.
  • Payme — payment processing; it receives invoice data, not your conversation.

We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Conversation and order data is kept while we serve you and afterwards for quality control. Records for completed, paid orders may be retained as long as the law requires.

To have your data deleted, write "Ma'lumotlarimni o'chiring" to the bot or call +998 20 021 60 40. We delete your conversation, profile and lead record within 30 days. See Data deletion.
