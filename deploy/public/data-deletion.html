Ma’lumotni o‘chirish

Maskan yordamchisi saqlagan ma’lumotlaringizni o‘chirish tartibi.

Qanday so‘rash mumkin

Ikkitasidan biri kifoya:

  • Yordamchiga (Telegram yoki WhatsApp orqali) «Ma’lumotlarimni o‘chiring» deb yozing;
  • yoki +998 20 021 60 40 raqamiga qo‘ng‘iroq qilib ayting.

Hech qanday ariza yoki hujjat talab qilinmaydi. So‘rov o‘sha raqamdan kelishi kifoya.

Nima o‘chiriladi

  • Yordamchi bilan bo‘lgan butun yozishmangiz;
  • profilingiz — ism, @username, telefon raqami;
  • murojaat yozuvingiz va siz aytgan qabr ma’lumotlari.

Nima qolishi mumkin

Bajarilgan va to‘langan buyurtmalar bo‘yicha hisob-kitob yozuvlari qonun talab qilgan muddatgacha saqlanadi. Ularda yozishma mazmuni bo‘lmaydi.

Muddat

So‘rovni olganimizdan keyin 30 kun ichida o‘chiramiz va bajarilgani haqida o‘sha yerning o‘zida xabar beramiz.

English

Data deletion

How to have the data held by the Maskan assistant deleted.

How to request it

Either write "Ma'lumotlarimni o'chiring" (delete my data) to the assistant on Telegram or WhatsApp, or call +998 20 021 60 40. No form or document is required — the request only has to come from the same number.

What is deleted

  • your entire conversation with the assistant;
  • your profile — name, @username, phone number;
  • your enquiry record and the grave details you gave us.

What may remain

Accounting records for completed, paid orders are kept for as long as the law requires. They contain no conversation content.

Timeframe

We delete within 30 days of the request and confirm it in the same chat.
