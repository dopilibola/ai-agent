Toshkent shahri va Toshkent viloyatidagi qabristonlar. Ro‘yxatda bo‘lmagan qabriston uchun buyurtma qabul qilinmaydi.

Terms of use

Assistant for Maskan, a grave-care service in Tashkent, on Telegram and WhatsApp. Last updated: 6 September 2026.

What the service is

The assistant advises on grave-care services, quotes prices from our catalogue and places orders. Replies are written by an AI; a human colleague continues the conversation when needed.

Service area

Cemeteries in Tashkent city and Tashkent region. Orders are not accepted for cemeteries outside that list.

Prices and payment

Prices come from the current catalogue, and the price at the moment of ordering is what is recorded. Payment is made through Payme. Once paid, the order is passed to the cemetery caretaker.

Cancellation

If the work has not started, call +998 20 021 60 40 to cancel.

Limits

  • The service covers human graves only.
  • The assistant gives no medical, legal or religious advice.
  • An AI can be wrong — prices and terms are final only once a member of staff confirms them.
